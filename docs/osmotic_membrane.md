# Osmotic Membrane: Oasis Integration with Legacy World

## Semi-Permeable Membrane Principle

KORNET Oases are not isolationist communes. They act as **cells with semi-permeable membranes** — exchanging resources with the outside world while keeping their internal cytoplasm (cybernetic logistics) impermeable to market entropy.

## Trade & Treasury Gateway

### Export (Outbound)
1. **Energy:** Oases with heli/wind/geo generators sell surplus energy to external grid at cost (joules + depreciation). Without speculator margin, price is 40-60% below market.
2. **Roboticized Production:** 3D printing, CNC, automated lines produce goods at pure material cost. No brand, advertising, or distributor markups.
3. **Open Patents / Software:** Oasis code and schematics published under GPLv3. External companies may use them, but modifications must be opened.

### Import (Inbound)
1. **Raw Materials:** During transition, Oasis not fully autonomous. Imports microchips, rare earths, complex equipment for fiat.
2. **Expertise:** Invited external specialists receive fiat compensation for consultations.

### Economic Dumping
```
Oasis Cost = Σ(Energy_Joules + Robot_Depreciation + Raw_Material_Cost)
Market Cost  = Oasis_Cost + Taxes + Rent + Labor + Speculator_Margin + Advertising

Result: Oasis goods 50-80% cheaper than market equivalents.
```

## Phase Transition

1. **Stage 1 (0-10,000 participants):** Islands. Small communities operating via app + local mesh.
2. **Stage 2 (10,000-100,000):** Oases. First physical spaces (leased land, co-work spaces) with full cybernetic management cycle.
3. **Stage 3 (100,000-1,000,000):** Clusters. Oases merge into regional networks. External enterprises begin integrating into protocol, contributing capacity in exchange for stable logistics and clean energy.
4. **Stage 4 (1,000,000+):** Core activation. Sufficient critical mass for full autonomy from legacy system.
